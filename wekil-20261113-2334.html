About wEKIL

wEKIL (ወኪል) means "the one who acts for you." It is an AI agent for Ethiopia: you speak, it sees your phone screen, decides the next tap, and does it — using free Google Gemini, with no Visa, no USD, and nothing to pay. Your key stays only on your phone. Version 0.8 · Chat with wEKIL like a friend, and ask it to do phone tasks — first version controls normal screens only; telebirr and CBE are left for a later phase.
